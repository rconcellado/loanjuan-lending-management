# Privacy Policy for LoanJuan Mobile Demo

**Effective Date: September 30, 2026**

LoanJuan Mobile Demo ("LoanJuan", "the App", "we", "our", or "us") is a lending management application intended for demonstration, testing, and authorized operational use.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and handled when using LoanJuan Mobile Demo.

## 1. Information We Process

Depending on the user's role and the LoanJuan environment being accessed, the App may process information such as:

- User account and authentication information
- User name, email address, role, and access information
- Borrower names and contact information
- Borrower addresses and lending records
- Loan applications and loan account information
- Repayment schedules
- Payment and transaction records
- Loan balances and charges
- Operational information related to lending activities
- Information required for reports, notifications, and borrower assignments

## 2. How We Use Information

Information processed through LoanJuan Mobile Demo may be used to:

- Authenticate authorized users
- Provide access to permitted application features
- Manage borrower and loan information
- Display and manage loan applications
- Record and display payments and repayment schedules
- Monitor loan balances and repayment activity
- Provide operational reports and summaries
- Support borrower assignments and notifications
- Maintain application security
- Diagnose and resolve technical issues

## 3. Data Sharing

LoanJuan Mobile Demo does not sell personal information.

Information processed by the App may be transmitted to the LoanJuan backend services required to provide the application's lending management functionality.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security and integrity of the application, its systems, or its users.

## 4. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect information handled through LoanJuan.

Access to application functionality is restricted to authorized users. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 5. Data Retention

Information is retained according to the requirements of the LoanJuan environment and the organization responsible for the underlying lending records.

Information used exclusively for demonstration or testing may be removed when it is no longer required.

## 6. Access, Correction, and Deletion Requests

Users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of applicable personal information by contacting the organization responsible for the LoanJuan environment or by using the contact information provided below.

Certain lending, payment, or transaction records may need to be retained when required for legitimate operational, legal, security, or record-keeping purposes.

## 7. Children's Privacy

LoanJuan Mobile Demo is not designed for use by children. The application is intended for authorized users performing lending management, administrative, demonstration, or testing activities.

## 8. Demo and Testing Environments

LoanJuan Mobile Demo may connect to demonstration or User Acceptance Testing (UAT) environments.

Users should avoid entering unnecessary real personal information into demonstration or testing environments and should follow the data-handling policies of the organization responsible for the applicable environment.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when LoanJuan functionality, legal requirements, or data-handling practices change.

Any updates will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
